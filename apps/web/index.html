Privacy

            
5KILL5 stores full Skill content for synchronization. Do not upload secrets, private keys, tokens, customer data, or other sensitive material.

            
Skill content is used for account storage, release manifest generation, device synchronization, and quality checks. It is not used for model training.

            
The platform manages and distributes Skill files. It does not write, rewrite, repair, or generate Skill content with third-party model APIs.
